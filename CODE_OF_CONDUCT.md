# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and don't harass or demean others. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors that violate this. Report concerns privately through the repository's *Security* tab (private reporting) or by contacting the maintainers.
